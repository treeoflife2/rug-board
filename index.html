Rug Board

Celebrities, influencers and public figures who used their audience to take money from followers through crypto: insider dumps, undisclosed paid promos, deleted promos and abandoned projects. Every record cites its sources. Amounts are estimates of what the person and their insiders took, drawn from public reporting, not what victims lost.

This list is not exhaustive, and it only covers what public investigations, filings and press have documented. Allegations stay allegations unless a settlement, conviction or admission says otherwise. Know an incident we missed? Submit it with sources.

Most of these records trace back to the investigations of ZachXBT. This board wouldn't exist without his work.
