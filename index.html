Rug Board

Celebrities, influencers and public figures who used their audience to take money from followers through crypto: insider dumps, undisclosed paid promos, deleted promos and abandoned projects. Every record cites its sources. Amounts are what the person and their insiders took, not what victims lost.

Most of these records trace back to the investigations of ZachXBT. This board wouldn't exist without his work.
